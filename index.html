    Settings

    Answer service

    Kept only in this browser on this phone. A free key comes from console.groq.com, under API Keys.

    Use a different service

    Works with any service that offers OpenAI-style /audio/transcriptions and /chat/completions and accepts calls from a web page.

    Answers
    Voice in your ear
    Microphone

    Room mode uses the unprocessed microphone and plays answers as ordinary media, which normally means phone microphone in, earbud out. Call mode behaves like a phone call, which on most phones moves the microphone to the earbud as well. If answers come out of the wrong place or the wrong microphone is live, try the other one.
